# Pilot 002 — “Bongo Popcorno” master prompt

## Objective

Create a more entertaining, better flowing Bongo short with an immediate visual hook, a simple silent cause-and-effect chain and a large readable payoff. Preserve Bongo and the yellow smiley bucket as the recognizable series anchors while avoiding the failed hand-held bucket shake from Pilot 001.

The complete silent story must read as:

`one hard corn kernel hits Bongo's tooth → the same kernel lands in the bucket → one whole banana follows → three taps on a planted bucket → the bucket rumbles by itself → a huge fountain of banana-shaped popcorn buries Bongo`

## Master prompt

Create a continuous 15-second vertical 9:16 high-quality 3D-cartoon sequence using the provided Bongo character reference as a strict identity source.

Preserve Bongo's exact light-brown shaggy fur, enormous round ears with peach interiors, asymmetric eyes, vivid cobalt-blue ring around the smaller right eye, one large front tooth, yellow banana peel worn on top of his head and exactly one blue sandal on his left foot. His right foot remains bare. Do not add clothing or accessories.

Keep the same warm garage workshop and the same glossy yellow plastic bucket with a simple black smiley face throughout. The bucket is an ordinary rigid object, never a speaking character. Do not add another bucket.

Use exactly one oversized golden corn kernel, approximately grape-sized so it stays readable on a phone screen, and exactly one intact yellow banana as the two ingredients. The intact ingredient banana is separate from the banana peel permanently worn on Bongo's head.

### Beat 1 — Tooth-PING hook, 0.0–3.0 seconds

Begin in the middle of the action, not with an establishing pause. In a tight three-quarter close-up, Bongo eagerly tries to bite the single oversized golden corn kernel. Within the first 0.8 seconds, the hard kernel strikes his firmly attached front tooth with one crisp comic PING and ricochets downward. The tooth does not loosen, bend or leave his mouth. Follow the same kernel just enough to show it landing inside the open yellow smiley bucket below. Bongo freezes for a short beat with ears raised and looks down at the bucket in confused surprise. No dialogue.

### Beat 2 — One banana follows, 3.0–7.0 seconds

Continue the movement direction into a medium shot. The same bucket remains in the same place with the one corn kernel hidden inside it. Bongo looks into the bucket, gets a silly idea, picks up exactly one intact yellow banana and clearly drops it through the open rim. The whole banana crosses the rim and disappears completely. Bongo gives one confident crooked grin. The final voice will later say “Bucketo,” but do not generate final dialogue in the video render.

### Beat 3 — Three taps and anticipation, 7.0–10.5 seconds

The bucket stays fully planted on the floor and initially does not move. Bongo never picks it up and never shakes it. With one paw, he gives the side or rim exactly three distinct taps: small, medium, strong. His paw visibly separates from the bucket after each contact. After the strong third tap, Bongo stops completely. Hold a clear half-second of silence and stillness. Then only the bucket begins to rumble by itself while staying in place. Bongo leans back and takes one wary half-step away.

### Beat 4 — Banana-popcorn eruption, 10.5–15.0 seconds

The camera makes a small motivated pullback to create room. A huge fountain of fluffy golden popcorn shaped like tiny curved bananas erupts upward from the same bucket. The popcorn rapidly piles around Bongo and buries his body up to his head and ears. The bucket remains visible at the base of the fountain. After the eruption settles, Bongo emerges enough to hold one banana-shaped popcorn piece, takes a satisfied bite and looks dryly proud. The final voice will later say “Bongo snacko,” but do not generate final dialogue in the video render. Hold the readable final image for at least 1.5 seconds.

## Direction and pacing

- The first comic failure must happen by 0.8 seconds.
- Use four clear narrative beats, not a rapid montage of tiny unrelated clips.
- Match movement direction and spatial placement across cuts.
- Use a tight hook, a calm medium ingredient shot, a slightly wider ritual shot and one wide payoff shot.
- Camera motion is allowed only for the brief kernel follow and the small payoff pullback.
- Give the half-second pre-eruption silence and final 1.5-second hold real screen time.
- Do not solve pacing later with speed changes above approximately 1.15×.

## Continuity and negative rules

- One Bongo only; never clone him.
- One yellow smiley bucket only; it remains in the same workshop floor area.
- One oversized golden corn kernel only before the transformation.
- One intact ingredient banana only; Bongo's head banana peel remains attached and unchanged.
- Bongo's front tooth remains fixed after the PING.
- The blue eye ring and single left sandal must remain visible and unchanged.
- The bucket is never lifted or hand-shaken.
- Do not show popcorn before the final eruption.
- Do not add a popcorn machine, bowl, package, spoon, text, captions, extra characters or replacement props.
- No teleportation, unexplained morphs, duplicate ingredients, loose teeth, explosions with fire or camera spinning.
- The final popcorn is fluffy food shaped like small curved bananas, not whole bananas and not yellow rocks.
- Prioritize first-view clarity, physical weight, comic timing and a strong final silhouette over frantic motion.

## Audio policy

Generate visual action and natural nonverbal sound only. Do not synthesize the final Bongo dialogue inside separate video shots. “Bucketo” and “Bongo snacko” will be created later as one continuous voice performance with a stable warm, slightly nasal, lightly raspy, mid-low register.
