## Critical observations

- Frame 01 is materially more scroll-stopping than the old fan setup. The lifted foot, snapped ears and visible kernel trajectory create an immediate mishap rather than a neutral establishing shot.
- The golden kernel is deliberately stylized and oversized. It reads more like a bright cartoon corn kernel than realistic maize, but the exaggeration is necessary for phone-scale visibility.
- Frame 02 has the clearest ingredient-to-bucket relationship produced so far in the project.
- Frame 03 successfully replaces hand-held side-to-side shaking with a planted bucket, a single tapping paw and self-generated vibration.
- Frame 04 reads as a large escalation. The popcorn pieces are curved and fluffy rather than literal whole bananas, keeping the food result distinct from the ingredient.
- Workshop placement and exact camera distance vary slightly, but lighting, materials, Bongo design and bucket design remain within storyboard continuity tolerance.

## Silent-story review

The four-frame sequence communicates the intended chain without dialogue:

`kernel hits tooth and falls toward bucket → whole banana follows → planted bucket is tapped and starts vibrating → banana-shaped popcorn buries Bongo`

The source images do not yet prove the exact three-tap motion or the full kernel/banana rim crossing; those remain mandatory motion gates for Veo. The storyboard does prove that each required state has a clear, readable composition.
