# Pilot 002 — continuity table

Reference image: `assets/character_master/bongo_reference.png`

## Permanent locks

| Element | Must remain constant | Automatic rejection if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bongo | Light-brown shaggy fur, huge round ears, peach inner ears, asymmetric eyes | Species, fur color, ear size or overall proportions drift |
| Right eye | Smaller eye surrounded by a vivid cobalt-blue ring | Ring disappears, changes eye or becomes face paint elsewhere |
| Mouth | Exactly one large attached front tooth | Tooth duplicates, bends, falls out or disappears after the PING |
| Head | One yellow banana peel resting on top | Peel becomes a whole banana, detaches or duplicates |
| Feet | Exactly one blue sandal on left foot; right foot bare | Sandal changes foot, duplicates or disappears |
| Bucket | One glossy yellow plastic bucket with one black smiley face | Second bucket appears, face changes, bucket becomes alive or soft |
| World | Same warm garage workshop, warm neutral light, uncluttered floor | Location or lighting changes without motivation |

## State progression

| Beat | Bongo | Bucket | Corn kernel | Ingredient banana | Popcorn |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1A: before PING | Holds one oversized golden kernel close to tooth | Empty, open, below Bongo | Exactly one, visible | Not present | None |
| 1B: after PING | Tooth intact; looks down | Same position | Same kernel lands inside | Not present | None |
| 2: banana drop | Crooked confident grin | Same position; kernel hidden inside | Hidden inside | Exactly one whole banana crosses rim and disappears | None |
| 3: three taps | Feet planted; one paw taps; then leans back | Stays on floor; still for taps, then rumbles itself | Hidden inside | Hidden inside | None |
| 4: eruption/tag | Buried to head/ears, then holds one snack piece | Still visible at bottom of pile | Transformed | Transformed | Large volume of small fluffy banana-shaped pieces |

## Motion locks

- Kernel trajectory is continuous: tooth contact → downward ricochet → bucket interior.
- Banana trajectory is continuous: Bongo's paw → across the rim → fully below the rim.
- The bucket stays in contact with the floor for the entire ritual.
- Tap rhythm is exactly three contacts with visible separation: small → medium → strong.
- The bucket begins self-rumbling only after the third tap and the half-second pause.
- The eruption originates only from the bucket opening.
- The payoff ends in a stable held composition for at least 1.5 seconds.

## Silent-story gate

The storyboard or animatic fails if a first-time viewer cannot answer all four questions without audio:

1. What hurt Bongo's tooth? — One hard corn kernel.
2. Where did the kernel and banana go? — Into the same yellow bucket.
3. What triggered the reaction? — Three taps followed by a pause.
4. What came out? — A large amount of banana-shaped popcorn.
