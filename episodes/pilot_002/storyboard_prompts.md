# Pilot 002 — storyboard image prompts

Generate all four images in vertical 9:16 using `assets/character_master/bongo_reference.png` as the strict character reference. Use the same warm garage workshop, the same floor marks, the same glossy yellow smiley bucket, the same Bongo proportions and the same warm neutral lighting in every frame.

## Frame 01 — tooth-PING hook

Tight three-quarter action close-up in a polished 3D-cartoon style. Preserve Bongo exactly from the reference: light-brown shaggy fur, enormous round ears, asymmetric eyes, vivid cobalt-blue ring around the smaller right eye, one firmly attached front tooth, banana peel on his head and exactly one blue sandal on the left foot. Capture the instant one oversized grape-sized golden corn kernel has just ricocheted off the front tooth with a clear comic impact pose. The tooth remains perfectly attached. The single kernel is visible in midair traveling downward toward the open yellow smiley bucket at the lower edge of frame. Bongo's ears are raised and his eyes track the kernel in startled confusion. Warm uncluttered garage background. Immediate scroll-stopping action, clean readable silhouette, no text.

Reject if: kernel duplicates, tooth loosens, blue eye ring disappears, head banana changes, a second bucket appears, or the kernel trajectory toward the bucket is unclear.

## Frame 02 — one banana enters

Medium three-quarter shot, same Bongo, workshop and exact yellow smiley bucket in the same floor position. The one corn kernel is already hidden inside. Bongo holds exactly one intact yellow ingredient banana just above the bucket opening at the moment before release; his paw, banana, bucket rim and downward path are all clearly visible. His head banana peel remains attached and visually distinct from the whole ingredient banana. Bongo wears a confident crooked grin. The bucket is large enough for the whole ingredient to disappear but remains proportionally consistent. Clean negative space, clear cause and effect, no text.

Reject if: the banana is already teleporting through the bucket, more than one ingredient banana appears, the head peel detaches, the bucket changes, the blue eye ring or left sandal disappears.

## Frame 03 — planted-bucket ritual

Medium-wide eye-level action shot, same warm garage, same Bongo and same yellow smiley bucket. The bucket sits flat and heavy on the floor, perfectly upright. Bongo stands beside it with both feet planted; exactly one left blue sandal and bare right foot are visible. One paw is raised just after a strong third tap, clearly separated from the bucket. Bongo's body leans slightly away in anticipation while the bucket shows only the first subtle vibration, with no lifting and no sideways hand-held shake. The ingredients remain fully hidden. Build tension through pose and spacing, not camera chaos. No popcorn yet, no text.

Reject if: Bongo holds or lifts the bucket, both arms shake it, popcorn appears early, the bucket slides, extra props appear, or identity features drift.

## Frame 04 — banana-popcorn payoff

Wide vertical 9:16 payoff composition in the same warm garage. The same yellow smiley bucket remains visible on the floor at the bottom center as the origin of a huge settling fountain and pile of fluffy golden popcorn pieces shaped like small curved bananas. Bongo is comically buried up to his head and enormous ears, with the vivid blue eye ring, one front tooth and head banana peel still clearly visible. One paw emerges holding a single banana-shaped popcorn piece near his mouth; his expression is dry, satisfied and proud. The eruption has just finished so the final image is stable, highly readable and visually funny. Strong silhouette, thumbnail-quality payoff, no text.

Reject if: the popcorn looks like whole bananas or rocks, the bucket disappears, Bongo is duplicated, the blue ring/tooth/head peel drift, fire appears, or the result is visually cluttered.

## Cross-frame acceptance gate

- Frame 01 must be funny and understandable at thumbnail size.
- Frames 01–02 must make the kernel-to-bucket and banana-to-bucket chain obvious.
- Frame 03 must visibly replace the rejected hand-held shaking with a planted, heavy bucket.
- Frame 04 must be a substantially bigger visual event than every prior frame.
- All four frames must look like moments from one continuous short, not unrelated generations.
