## A — Weird-cute default

Small original fantasy-creature voice; lightly raspy and nasal, warm, playful, slightly slow and melodically expressive. Confidently foolish, never childlike or shrill. Rounded vowels. Finish “Bongo fixo” short, dry and proud.

## B — Tiny body, absurdly deep

Very low, rounded, deadpan and slow; overconfident but comedic, not threatening. Minimal emotion except satisfied pride. Finish “Bongo fixo” as a compact dry stamp.

## C — Bouncy with deadpan ending

Quick, energetic and rhythmically musical; slightly nasal, playful, not shrill or childish. Make the Banga escalation catchy. Contrast with a short, calm and proud “Bongo fixo.”
