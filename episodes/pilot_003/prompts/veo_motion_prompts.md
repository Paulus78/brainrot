# Pilot 003 — Veo motion prompts

The original frame-pair method below is retained for audit, but it was rejected after the first test because the anchors did not depict the same composition. The active workflow uses `bongo.png` only as an identity image element and lets Veo stage one coherent shot. Two variants are generated per prompt; the better one moves to QC.

## Active Shot 01 — giant corn cob slurp

Reference element: `assets/character_master/bongo_reference.png`

Use the attached image only as the exact character identity reference for Bongo: same brown fur, huge ears, asymmetric eyes, vivid blue ring around the smaller right eye, one front tooth, banana peel on his head, and exactly one blue sandal on his left foot. Create one continuous funny 8-second vertical 9:16 3D-cartoon shot in a warm garage workshop. Static eye-level camera. From the very first frame, one absurdly huge normal yellow corn cob is already stuck upright halfway inside one small yellow bucket with a simple black smiley face. Bongo urgently pushes down on the top of the cob with both hands and his whole body; his feet slide, knees bend, belly and cheeks squash, ears and fur lag naturally. After real resistance, the bucket suddenly sucks the complete corn cob straight down in one fast continuous SCHLORP until it disappears fully below the rim. Bongo loses balance and falls chest-first over the bucket, then slowly lifts his head and stares into it in stunned silence while the bucket bulges once and settles. Make the physical comedy broad and readable, with anticipation, effort, sudden release, follow-through and a short reaction hold. Only Bongo, one corn cob and one bucket. No tiny kernels, no banana-corn hybrid, no popcorn yet, no repeated action, no morphing, no teleporting, no dialogue, no text, no subtitles, no camera movement. Add rubbery effort squeaks, one loud SCHLORP and a soft bucket BOING.

## Shot 02 — one big bucket slam

Start: `keyframes/shot_02_slam_anticipation.png`  
End: `keyframes/shot_02_slam_impact.png`

Animate one complete physical double-palm slam. Begin with Bongo crouched and both hands high. He inhales, drops his hips, then accelerates both arms downward together. Both palms strike the bucket rim simultaneously exactly once. At contact the bucket briefly compresses shorter and wider, the floor dust puffs outward, Bongo's elbows bend and his whole torso overshoots, then rebounds. His ears, fur and banana peel lag behind and snap upward on the recoil. He freezes for a half beat, startled by his own strength, while the bucket remains planted and settles back toward its normal shape. Do not loop or repeat the hit. No dissolve, teleport, extra tap, bucket lift, explosion, popcorn, duplicate limbs, text, dialogue or camera movement. Preserve Bongo and bucket exactly.
