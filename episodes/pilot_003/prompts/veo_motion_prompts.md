# Pilot 003 — Veo motion prompts

These prompts animate between fixed start/end anchors. They prioritize physical motion and readable causality over dialogue, camera tricks or decorative action.

## Shot 01 — banana-corn tooth ping and bucket fall

Start: `keyframes/shot_01_bananacorn_hook_start.png`  
End: `keyframes/shot_01_hook_end.png`

One continuous physically animated 8-second 3D-cartoon shot between the provided start and end frames, vertical 9:16, static eye-level camera. Preserve the exact same Bongo, warm garage and yellow smiley bucket. 0.0-1.3s: Bongo leans his whole upper body forward and bites the middle of the single oversized banana-corn hybrid; cheeks compress, ears lag naturally, hands grip both ends. 1.3-2.2s: his one front tooth makes a sharp metallic PING and the hybrid object springs out of his hands; Bongo recoils backward with real weight, arms and ears following through. 2.2-4.0s: the complete large banana-corn object rotates once through a clear unobstructed downward arc directly into the open bucket; keep it visible the entire way, then it passes fully below the rim and disappears inside. 4.0-6.2s: the bucket flexes once from the impact and settles; Bongo catches his balance and leans over it, staring down in confused surprise. 6.2-8.0s: hold the curious end pose with subtle breathing and fur motion. Natural continuous in-between motion, anticipation, contact, recoil, follow-through and settling; no crossfades, no pose dissolves, no teleporting, no hidden object path. Keep one banana-corn object only. No loose kernel, separate banana, popcorn, extra character, text, subtitles, dialogue, duplicate limbs, extra sandal or camera move. Add only a crisp tooth PING, soft whoosh and bucket THUNK as diegetic sound.

## Shot 02 — one big bucket slam

Start: `keyframes/shot_02_slam_anticipation.png`  
End: `keyframes/shot_02_slam_impact.png`

Animate one complete physical double-palm slam. Begin with Bongo crouched and both hands high. He inhales, drops his hips, then accelerates both arms downward together. Both palms strike the bucket rim simultaneously exactly once. At contact the bucket briefly compresses shorter and wider, the floor dust puffs outward, Bongo's elbows bend and his whole torso overshoots, then rebounds. His ears, fur and banana peel lag behind and snap upward on the recoil. He freezes for a half beat, startled by his own strength, while the bucket remains planted and settles back toward its normal shape. Do not loop or repeat the hit. No dissolve, teleport, extra tap, bucket lift, explosion, popcorn, duplicate limbs, text, dialogue or camera movement. Preserve Bongo and bucket exactly.

## Shot 03 — continuous eruption and snack payoff

Start: `keyframes/shot_03_eruption_start.png`  
End: `keyframes/shot_03_payoff_end.png`

Animate one continuous escalating banana-popcorn eruption. Start with the first few curved banana-shaped popcorn pieces bubbling above the bucket. The flow rapidly builds into a thick upward fountain; pieces spin, collide, fall under gravity and pile around Bongo's legs while he recoils, shields his face and staggers backward with real body weight. The fountain grows enough to bury his torso but never duplicates the bucket or character. The eruption then eases; Bongo emerges through the fresh pile, shakes loose popcorn, grabs exactly one piece, tastes it and gives a tiny satisfied nod into the final pose. Continuous motion throughout, with real trajectories and settling pieces; no still-image morph, crossfade, teleportation, text, subtitles, extra character or camera move.
